Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 14, 2026 · SkinWatch Version 10.0

SkinWatch ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your personal information and privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when using the SkinWatch mobile and web applications.

1. Camera & Facial Photography Data

SkinWatch features on-device AI Biometric Facial Scans, Redness (Erythema) Trackers, Acne Zone Mapping, and Packaging Ingredient OCR.

  • On-Device & User Isolation: Photos captured for acne tracking or label analysis are processed to compute cosmetic scores (Dawson Erythema Index, comedogenic ratings).
  • No Third-Party Sale: We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal photos or biometric data to third parties, cosmetic brands, or advertising networks.
  • Storage: Images are stored locally on your device (browser LocalStorage / app sandbox) and synchronized exclusively with your authenticated user partition for your private tracking history.

2. Location & Environmental Telemetry

  • Weather & UV Index: We use coarse/fine location coordinates solely to query real-time ambient ultraviolet index (UV), relative humidity, and air quality index (AQI) from Google Weather / Open-Meteo services.
  • No Background Tracking: Location is only requested when refreshing climate-driven skincare routine recommendations.

3. Non-Diagnostic Health & Cosmetic Disclaimer

SkinWatch is a cosmetic wellness and ingredient education tool. It does not provide medical diagnosis, dermatological prescription, or treatment of clinical conditions. All formulations, ratings (0–5 comedogenicity), and zone statistics are for personal educational tracking.

4. Data Security & Encryption

All communication between the application and backend servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). User databases are isolated on secure server environments.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You can clear your local scan history, logs, or delete your profile directly from the app settings, or request permanent deletion by contacting our support team.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy inquiries, please reach out to us at bala@pristinemedsolutions.com.
